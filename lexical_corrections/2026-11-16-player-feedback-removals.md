# Exact-surface removals after player feedback: pitiz, aló, asz, rézi, nyí, fűk, esz

Prepared and published on 16 November 2026 at Fredrik's request, after a native
review, to the production Hungarian standard, classic and mixed dictionaries
(lexicon versions 44, 35 and 31).

On 15 November 2026 a new player reported (ABCx3 beta feedback) that opponents
played meaningless words. In the game concerned, `pitiz`, `aló` and `asz` were
accepted only because they are standalone Magyar Ispell headwords. The native
reviewer (Péter) confirmed on 16 November that all three should go, and asked
for four more surfaces he had met to be removed: `rézi`, `nyí`, `fűk` and `esz`.

Only these seven exact surfaces are removed, plus the 35 nominal inflections of
`rézi` (`rézit`, `réziben`, `réziek` …), which only `rézi` licensed: 42 forms in
total. Related dictionary forms stay,
notably `pitizik`, `pitizett`, `pitizni`, `aszik`, `aszok`, `füvek` and `réz`.

## Evidence

Webcorpus 2.2 counts (complete, quality-40, quality-8, quality-4) and source:

| Surface | Counts | Source and note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pitiz | 0, 0, 0, 0 | `pitiz/932` beside `pitizik/922`; the dictionary form is `pitizik` |
| aló | 149, 147, 112, 73 | bare headword; hits are likely `haló`/`alól` typos or foreign text |
| asz | 907, 797, 296, 115 | bare headword, stem of `aszik`; hits are likely `az`/`ász` typos |
| rézi | 7, 7, 6, 5 | attested derivation of `réz`; not an established adjective |
| nyí | 212, 185, 45, 13 | bare headword `nyí/178` |
| fűk | 2, 2, 0, 0 | inflection generated for `fű`, whose plural is `füvek` |
| esz | 1366, 1204, 509, 197 | stem of `eszik`; hits are likely `az`/`ez` typos |

The player's other unusual words in the same game (`folysz`, `veknid`, `örege`,
`sülsz`) are ordinary inflections and stay.
