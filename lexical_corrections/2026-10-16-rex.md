# Exact headword correction: rex

Prepared locally on 16 October 2026. Publication remains a separate operation.

Add the exact lowercase surface `rex`. It is listed as a headword by
Akadémiai Kiadó in both its Hungarian explanatory dictionary and its dictionary
of foreign words. Its Hungarian common-noun use includes a billiards-like
table game. This admission does not rely on the Latin word for a king or on a
proper name.

Primary publisher evidence:

- <https://www.szotar.net/magyar/rex> lists `rex` in **Magyar értelmező
  kéziszótár** (English: **Concise Explanatory Dictionary of Hungarian**) and
  **Idegen szavak és kifejezések szótára** (English: **Dictionary of Foreign
  Words and Expressions**).
- The explanatory headword has the permanent address
  <https://www.szotar.net/szocikk/lerte022003-55301-rex/>. Full entry text requires
  a subscription; the public publisher search establishes the headword, not
  access to its complete definitions.
- <https://www.szotar.net/angol/carom> also indexes `rex` in the publisher's
  Hungarian-English dictionary.

Neither pinned Magyar Ispell nor morphdb.hu recognizes this surface, so normal
source generation cannot recover it. The portable gameplay override and the
parent correction register both admit it explicitly. The evidence builder's
existing `reviewed_surface_addition` path admits the exact word without
licensing a new inflection family. Definition promotion self-maps it to `rex`.

The classic Hungarian tile inventory cannot spell X and must continue to
exclude this surface. The standard dictionary and mixed tile inventory can
represent it; mixed spelling is R | E | X.

No change to abbreviation policy or the unrelated disputed `box` exception is
part of this correction.
