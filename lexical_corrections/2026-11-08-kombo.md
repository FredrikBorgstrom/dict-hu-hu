# Reviewed-surface correction: kombó

Prepared and published on 8 November 2026 to the existing production Hungarian standard,
classic and mixed dictionaries, at Fredrik's request after a player report
(ABCx3 beta feedback, 7 November 2026). The player explained that `kombó`
(English: "combo") is common for a combination or pairing, for example
`zokni-szandál kombó` (English: "socks-and-sandals combo"), and that a
`gitár kombó` (English: "guitar combo") is an amplifier and speaker in one.

Admit 209 exact reviewed surfaces through the existing reviewed-surface path:
`kombó`, 206 ordinary nominal inflections, the attested adjective `kombós`
and the compound `gitárkombó`.

## Evidence

Neither pinned analyser knows the lemma: Magyar Ispell and morphdb.hu return
no analysis for `kombó`, `kombók`, `kombót`, `kombós` or `gitárkombó` (both
analyse `kombi`). The Research Centre for Linguistics' spelling adviser,
queried through the backend's `HungarianSpellingAdviserProvider` on
8 November 2026, also reports all 209 surfaces as unknown; it recognizes
`fotó`, `kombi` and `fázókat` in the same session, so this is a coverage gap
in the adviser, not a failed lookup. The adviser is therefore not evidence
for this correction, unlike `fázókat`.

The admission rests on the pinned Webcorpus 2.2 frequency list instead.
Counts are (complete, quality-40, quality-8, quality-4):

Forty further admitted surfaces occur in the complete corpus (for example
`kombóra` 83, `kombóban` 43, `kombónak` 22, `kombóhoz` 10). The lemma's 16
quality-4 occurrences exceed the ten-occurrence gate the build already uses
for morphdb.hu headwords missing from Magyar Ispell.

The inflected surfaces are the complete set of nominal forms the current
dictionary already accepts for the analogous back-vowel noun `fotó` (585
surfaces mapped to that lemma), applied to `kombó`. Derivational branches of
that family (`-s`, `-z`, `-szerű`, `-nyi`, adjectival `-i` with `-ak`, `-an`,
`-bb`) are excluded; of the derived forms only `kombós` is admitted, on its own
corpus evidence. Forms longer than the unchanged ten-character build limit are
omitted. The full list and the adviser responses are archived in
`/Users/fredrik/Documents/abcx3_bun/artifacts/Kombo Dictionary Publication 2026-11-08/`.

## Scope

Record the surfaces in both the portable `gameplay_overrides.json` and the
parent `_community_overrides/hungarian_hu_hu_ispell/` decision register.
This approves exact surfaces only. It does not license other derivations
(for example the verb `kombózik`), change any reviewed removal, alphabet,
length limit or automated-player vocabulary, or relax a general gate.

No surface contains a Hungarian digraph or ambiguous letter sequence. Both
tile modes spell the lemma K | O | M | B | Ó. Definition lookup maps each new
surface to itself, as for other reviewed additions without a source analysis.
