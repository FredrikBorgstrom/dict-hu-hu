# Reviewed-surface correction: kresz

Prepared and published on 22 November 2026 to the production Hungarian standard,
classic and mixed dictionaries (lexicon versions 47, 38 and 34), at Fredrik's
request after a player report
(ABCx3 beta feedback, 22 November 2026). The player wrote that `kresz` is a
well-known common word that the game refused. `KRESZ` is the initialism of
*Közúti Közlekedés Szabályai*, the Hungarian Highway Code; in lowercase it is
used as an ordinary noun (`tudja a kreszt`, "knows the Highway Code";
`kreszvizsga`, "driving-theory test"). Fredrik decided that this everyday
initialism belongs in the list, as English keeps everyday initialisms such as
HR and TV.

Admit 239 exact reviewed surfaces through the existing reviewed-surface path:
`kresz`, its ordinary nominal inflections and the compound `kreszvizsga` with
its short inflections. 149 surfaces have up to 10 letters and enter the text
lists and graphs; 90 have 11 or 12 letters and enter only the 12-letter graph
sources (see Length below).

## Evidence

Neither pinned analyser knows the lemma. The admission rests on the pinned
Webcorpus 2.2 frequency list. Counts are (complete, quality-40, quality-8,
quality-4), lowercase only:

| Surface | Counts |
| --- | --- |
| kresz | 481, 463, 312, 184 |
| kreszt | 107, 103, 57, 15 |
| kreszben | 17, 17, 13, 6 |
| kreszből | 15, 15, 14, 9 |
| kresznek | 8, 8, 6, 1 |
| kreszre | 7, 7, 5, 0 |
| kreszvizsga | 7, 7, 7, 5 |
| kreszvizsgán | 4, 3, 3, 3 |

The capitalized initialism is far more frequent (`KRESZ` 2,615, `KRESZ-t` 224,
`KRESZ-ben` 116), confirming the word; only the lowercase spellings above are
counted as evidence. The lemma's 184 quality-4 occurrences exceed the
ten-occurrence gate the build uses for morphdb.hu headwords missing from Magyar
Ispell.

The inflected surfaces are the nominal forms the current dictionary already
accepts for the analogous front-vowel noun `szesz` ("spirits"), applied to
`kresz` (`szesszel` → `kresszel`, `szesszé` → `kresszé`). Derivational
branches of that family (`szeszes…`, `szeszez…`, `szeszmentes…`,
`szeszfélék`) are excluded. For the compound, the forms accepted for `vizsga`
that keep `kreszvizsga…` within 12 letters are admitted (`kreszvizsgát`,
`kreszvizsgán`, `kreszvizsgák`, `kreszvizsgám`, `kreszvizsgád`,
`kreszvizsgái`, `kreszvizsgáé`); the adjectives `kreszvizsgai` and
`kreszvizsgás` are excluded. Corpus look-alikes such as `kresztül` and
`kresztöt` are misspellings of `keresztül` and `keresztet`, not inflections,
and are not admitted. The full list is archived in
`/Users/fredrik/Documents/abcx3_dictionaries/artifacts/Kresz Addition 2026-11-22/`.

## Length

This is the first reviewed addition with surfaces longer than the 10-letter
text-list cap. `reviewed_additions.py` therefore accepts reviewed surfaces of up
to 12 letters (the graph maximum since 17 November 2026), and each build keeps
only those within its own cap: the 10-letter build leaves the 90 longer forms
out, and `build_long_graph_sources.py` admits them into the `*_max12.*` graph
sources.

## Scope

Record the surfaces in both the portable `gameplay_overrides.json` and the
parent `_community_overrides/hungarian_hu_hu_ispell/` decision register.
This approves exact surfaces only. It does not license other derivations
(for example `kreszes`) or compounds (for example `kresztábla`), other
initialisms, or relax a general gate.

`kresz` contains the digraph `sz`: both tile modes spell it K | R | E | SZ, and
the mixed lexicon also keeps the split spelling. `kresszel` uses the shortened
double S | SZ. Definition lookup maps each new surface to itself, as for other
reviewed additions without a source analysis.
