# Exact-surface removals after native review: gyűl, fűl

Prepared and published on 23 November 2026 at Fredrik's request, after a native
review, to the production Hungarian standard, classic and mixed dictionaries
(lexicon versions 48, 39 and 35).

The native reviewer (Péter) said that `gyűl` ("gathers") and `fűl` ("heats")
are ik-verbs whose dictionary form must carry `-ik`: `gyűlik`, `fűlik`. The
bare third-person forms without `-ik` are not standard. Fredrik approved the
removal on 23 November 2026.

Only these two exact surfaces are removed. `gyűlik`, `fűlik` and every other
form of both verbs stay (`gyűlt`, `gyűlnek`, `gyűlni`, `fűlt`, `fűlnek`,
`fűlni` …), as do the prefixed verbs.

## Evidence

| Surface | Counts | Source and note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| gyűl | 395, 388, 264, 155 | bare `gyűl` and `gyűl/1041` beside `gyűlik/928`; the dictionary form is `gyűlik` (3640, 3635, 2918, 1527) |
| fűl | 43, 42, 16, 11 | bare `fűl` and `fűl/1041` beside `fűlik/928`; the dictionary form is `fűlik` (248, 248, 174, 125) |

## Scope

Exact surfaces only, recorded in the portable `gameplay_overrides.json` and in
the parent `_community_overrides/hungarian_hu_hu_ispell/` decision register.
The prefixed bare forms `egybegyűl`, `felgyűl`, `meggyűl`, `odagyűl`,
`összegyűl`, `kifűl` and `átfűl` are still accepted; they were not part of the
review and are left for the reviewer to judge.
